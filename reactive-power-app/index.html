STEP 5 Reactive capability vs grid voltage

Grid codes require reactive support across a voltage band, not just at nominal. Below 1.00 p.u. the inverter is current-limited, so its apparent power shrinks with voltage (S = V × Srated); above 1.00 p.u. it holds rated apparent power. With active power dispatched first (P-priority), whatever capability remains is available as reactive power.
